How single-donor allocation works:
Rank 1 is the primary match recommendation; it is not a confirmed allocation. Candidates ranked 2 and below serve as the prioritized standby queue in case the primary candidate is medically unavailable, uncontactable, or declines. If candidates tie on all clinical and demographic tiebreakers, the result is flagged for coordinator review. Final allocation requires authorised medical/coordinator confirmation. This is a synthetic-data academic prototype, not a clinical decision system.
